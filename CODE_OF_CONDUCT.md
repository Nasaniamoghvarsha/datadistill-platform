# Code of conduct

We want DataDistill to be a friendly, respectful place to work on, for everyone who takes part.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Accept feedback the same way.
- Respect other people's privacy. Never share recordings, names, voices or documents of people who did not agree to it.
- Keep partner and personal data out of the repository, issues and pull requests.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in any form.

If someone behaves in a way that breaks these rules, tell a maintainer. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and
contributions that break them, and may ask a person to stop taking part.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
